IvanaFin

A Jellyfin app for your TV, with big-tile home screen, full-screen film and show pages and a TV-style music library. You need a Jellyfin server to use it.

Fire TV

Fire TV Stick, Fire TV Cube, Fire TV televisions

Download for Fire TV
  1. Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore (search "Downloader").
  2. Settings › My Fire TV › About: press OK on the device name 7 times. Then Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps › turn on Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader and type chad2bad.github.io/IvanaFin-Android/fire - the download starts by itself.
  4. Choose Install, then Open. Pick your server, sign in, done.

Google TV / Android TV

Google TV Streamer, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield, Sony / TCL / Philips TVs

Download for Google TV
  1. Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Google Play Store on the TV.
  2. Settings › System › About: press OK on Android TV OS build 7 times. Then Settings › Apps › Security & restrictions › Unknown sources › turn on Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader and type chad2bad.github.io/IvanaFin-Android/tv - the download starts by itself.
  4. Choose Install, then Open. Pick your server, sign in, done.

Smaller download for 64-bit boxes (Shield, Google TV Streamer): 64-bit version.

Updates

IvanaFin checks for new versions itself and tells you when one is out - install it the same way.

Good to know

The menu names above can differ a little between TV models. Phones and tablets are not supported - IvanaFin is made for a TV remote. Artist pages ask Deezer for each artist's popular songs (the artist's name is sent to Deezer); Star Wars pictures and update checks come from GitHub.

IvanaFin is free software (GPL-2.0), built on Wholphin and Jellyfin. Source code and all versions: github.com/CHAD2BAD/IvanaFin-Android.
